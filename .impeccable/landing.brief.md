# Surface brief: landing page (index.html)

Mode: Persuade

## Direction contract
THESIS: The page is the issue. Instead of describing the gift in cards and lists, the visitor holds it: a fashion cover opens and its 16 pages turn as they scroll. Refuses the ivory-with-hairlines brochure.
OWN-WORLD: Burgundy drenched fields, deep burgundy studio stage, ivory paper pages, khaki halftone photo slots, giant Frank Ruhl display at magazine-cover scale, Heebo body, folios and running heads as real page furniture.
STORY: See the cover (understand), watch it open spread by spread (desire), price as a cover price and a cut-out coupon (trust), questionnaire to WhatsApp (act).
FIRST VIEWPORT: Burgundy; three-line ivory headline at cover scale on the right; the 3D cover tilting on the left with a gloss sheen; ivory primary CTA in view.
SIGNATURE: Scroll-driven 3D flip-through of the 16-page sample issue with a live table of contents.
RISK: Heavy DOM and 3D; single-page flip on phones; reduced motion and no-JS still read.
