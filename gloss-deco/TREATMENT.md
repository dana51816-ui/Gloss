# Studio GLOSS · "הערב, על השער" (Tonight, on the Cover)

Style: **Art Deco** (Lemo-Opuscar). Paid-social spot for Studio GLOSS, the personal 16-page magazine gift.
Format: vertical 1080×1920, 24 fps, 32.5 s = 13 bars of a slow foxtrot at 96 BPM (beat 0.625 s = 15 frames, bar 2.5 s). Hebrew, RTL.
No voice-over (paid social plays muted); the type cards and the dial carry the message.
Owner's notes for this version: no loud red; far more motion; palette burgundy, powder pink, cream.

## Palette (the style's triad, in the brand's colours)

- Ground: warm lacquer black with an oxblood cast `#140608`; ray wedges `#1F0A0E`, never pure black.
- Metal: **rose gold** (the style allows rose gold as its metal): bands `#5E2F2B → #B9806F → #F3CDBB → #FFF1E8 → #B9806F → #5E2F2B`.
- Jewel tone for secondary surfaces: burgundy `#6B0F1E` (door leaves, plaques), shade `#3A0610`, light `#9A2238`.
- **The one accent, reserved for the protagonist: powder pink `#FFC9DC`**: the magazine cover, and only it.
- Ivory/cream `#F8F2E7` for type and paper.
- Logos as delivered: burgundy logo (with its gold stroke) on cream plaques, pink logo on lacquer.

## Type (Hebrew stand-ins of the same kind, all OFL)

- Titles: **Frank Ruhl Libre** 900 (the brand's display serif), drawn into stepped gold title bars, with a sheen sweep.
- Numbers, dial, plaques: **Bellefair** (high-contrast, thin, deco numerals).
- Cards and small labels: **Heebo** 300/500 (in the place of Josefin Sans).

## 1. Three candidate structures

1. **The premiere.** A gala unveiling: the title promises "tonight, on the cover", four doors open on four rooms of the issue (interview, playlist, recipe, album), a page dial counts the issue forward, the final doors open on the cover, lit by 16 bulbs, one per page. Then the cover joins a kaleidoscope of other covers (one programme, many people) and the end card.
2. **The exhibition.** Five pavilions of a world's fair, one per section of the magazine, the camera walking an axis between them.
3. **The lights go out.** Open on the finished cover lit like a marquee, then follow the lights going out page by page as the story is told backwards.

**Chosen: 1.** It is the gift's real emotion (a person treated like a star for one night), it uses the style's most kinetic moves (threshold doors, counted light, dials, sheen, kaleidoscope) so something moves in every frame, and the reveal lands as a payoff. Structure 2 is slower (camera travel between pavilions); 3 ends in the dark, wrong for a gift.

## 2. Logline and arc

Tonight one ordinary person gets the cover: four doors open on the rooms of their issue, the last doors open on the cover itself, lit page by page.

- Setup (0–5 s): a rose-gold line engraves itself down the centre, a sunburst unfolds, bulbs chase: "הערב, על השער:".
- Development (5–15 s): four doors, four rooms; the dial counts pages 04 → 14.
- Turn (15–22.5 s): a drum roll, near silence, the doors open on the powder-pink cover; 16 bulbs light, one per page; "אמא." is engraved; sheen.
- Widening (22.5–27.5 s): the cover spins into a kaleidoscope of other covers that lock into a star.
- Ending (27.5–32.5 s): end card on a cream plaque: logo, slogan, price, a bulb-lit WhatsApp button.

## 3. Benchmark

- 1930s travel and liner posters (Cassandre-like grammar: giant geometry, airbrush inside hard edges), gilded title sequences where gold line draws on black, cinema premiere marquees.
- Learn: symmetry and the centre axis, mechanical easing with a small settle, light arriving in counted order, the sheen as the punctuation of every reveal.
- Don't take: any poster composition, real building, typeface design, melody, or the demo's hotel, bellboy, lifts or story.
