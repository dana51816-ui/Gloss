## Platform

web

## Stack

Static: a single index.html with inline CSS and JS, deployed to Netlify, plus gloss-logo.png.

## Users

Israelis (Hebrew, RTL) buying a personal gift for someone they love: a partner, parents, grandparents, a friend. Most arrive on a phone, often from Instagram or a shared link, looking for a gift that feels more meaningful than a card or an object.

## Product Purpose

Studio GLOSS produces a personal 16-page magazine-style issue about one person or a couple, ordered as a gift for a birthday, anniversary, a year together, for parents, grandparents or friends. The buyer fills a questionnaire and sends photos; the studio writes, edits and designs the issue. Success for the landing page: the visitor understands the idea in seconds, falls in love with the gift, and opens a WhatsApp conversation.

## Positioning

A real editorial product, not a template: every word is written about the person being celebrated, with a magazine's structure (cover, exclusive interview, top 5, playlist, recipe, crossword and quiz, album, greetings, back cover).

## Operating Context

Order flow: WhatsApp message > questionnaire + photos > studio writes, designs, sends for approval > customer receives a ready file to send and print. Sections can be swapped to fit the story.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Studio GLOSS. Logo file gloss-logo.png (burgundy serif wordmark with khaki brush stroke); never recolor or distort.
- Palette pinned by the owner: deep burgundy #6B0F1E, khaki #8C7A55 (small text #6E5E3E), ivory #F8F2E7. No blue at all.
- Type pinned: bold Hebrew serif display (Frank Ruhl Libre or similar), clean sans body (Heebo or Assistant).
- Look: luxurious, editorial, fashion-magazine. Must feel "wow", never generic or AI-looking.
- Banned: colorful gradients, emoji, cards with a colored side stripe, generic icons, em dashes.
