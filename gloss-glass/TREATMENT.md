# Studio GLOSS · "16 עמודים של אור" (Sixteen Pages of Light)

Style: **Glass Product Render** (Lemo-Opuscar). Paid-social launch film for Studio GLOSS, the personal 16-page magazine gift.
Format: vertical 1080×1920, 24 fps, 32 s at 90 BPM (beat 0.6667 s = 16 frames, bar 2.667 s, 12 bars). Hebrew, RTL. No voice-over.
Owner's notes so far: the flat versions looked low-end; wanted richer design, real motion, no loud red; palette burgundy, powder pink, cream.

## The hero object (fictional)

**The Issue**: a thick slab of optical glass in magazine proportions (21 × 28 cm, 24 mm thick), bevelled edges. Inside it, 16 opaque printed pages (paper cards 0.3 mm) stacked like a jewel set in resin; the front one is the cover ("אמא.", the GLOSS masthead). A satin rose-metal spine bar on the binding edge carries a light guide; light pulses travel along it.
Invisible thing it does, made visible: *it holds a whole story about one person*. The story is light travelling through the pages.

## Colour

Neutral world: black, greys, white strip reflections, rose-metal satin. **One accent family reserved for light: powder pink → rose** (`#FFC9DC` → `#E87A9A`), in the comet pulses, the caustic rings and the glint on titles. The printed pages carry the brand (burgundy `#6B0F1E`, cream `#F8F2E7`, powder pink) as printed matter inside the glass, never as light.

## Type

Hebrew stand-in for the thin wide grotesk: **Heebo 200/300** for titles (very large, wide tracking avoided in Hebrew; size and weight carry it), Heebo 400 for the frosted subtitle slab, small tracked Latin caps for "STUDIO GLOSS". Titles are cards, centred in the upper third, with a diagonal glint in sync with the sweep.

## 1. Three candidate structures

1. **Frost to clear, then the exploded issue.** A frosted slab in the dark; a sweep finds its edges; the frost clears and the cover appears inside; the slab opens into its 16 pages floating apart on an axis; light runs through them; silence; snap shut on the downbeat; caustic ring on the floor; end on the name.
2. **Material stack.** Remove one layer per beat (glass, cover, pages) until a single line of text remains.
3. **A day in light.** The same slab at morning, noon, night as the strip kit rotates.

**Chosen: 1.** It shows the product (16 pages) as the spectacle, gives a reveal (frost → clear), a suspended hover (exploded view) and a hit (snap) on the beat: the full arc of a premium launch film. 2 is colder; 3 has no turn.

## 2. Logline and arc

A cold slab of glass clears to reveal a cover with one name on it, then opens into the sixteen pages written about her.
Setup: the unknown dark object (0–5). Reveal: frost clears, the cover (5–11). Spectacle: exploded pages with light running through (11–20). Turn: silence, snap (20–21.3). Ending: monument, title, end card (21.3–32).

## 3. Benchmark

Premium launch films for watches, perfume and audio (grammar: contour drawn by a sweep in the dark, slow orbits, macro rack focus, cuts on the beat, ultra-thin type). Learn the pacing and restraint; copy no silhouette, typeface, sound or colour template, and name none of them.
