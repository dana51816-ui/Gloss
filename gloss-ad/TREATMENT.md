# Studio GLOSS · "מגיע שער" (A Cover Is Due)

Style: **Swiss Motion Graphics** (Lemo-Opuscar). Paid-social spot for Studio GLOSS, the personal 16-page magazine gift.
Format: vertical 1080×1920, 24 fps, 32 s (16 bars at 120 BPM, one beat = 12 frames exactly). Hebrew, right to left.
No voice-over: paid social plays muted first, so the type carries the whole message; sound rewards those who unmute.

## 1. Three candidate structures

1. **The list that gets cut.** The usual gift ideas stack up as a tidy list, then a knife deletes them one by one. On the empty page one red square arrives off the grid: the person. The magazine's programme builds a cover around it, the camera pulls out to a wall of issues, push back in, end card.
2. **A timetable.** A 16-row grid, one row per page of the issue, fills with the sections (interview, top 5, playlist, recipe...) beat by beat until the grid folds into a cover.
3. **The finished cover, dismantled.** Open on a complete cover, then strip it into its rules (grid, masthead, cover lines, headline) to show the craft, rebuild on the downbeat.

**Chosen: 1.** It hooks in the first frame with something every buyer recognises (the generic gift), gives a turn the audience feels (deletion, silence, one red square), and lets the style's two strongest moves carry the product: the knife cut (reject the ordinary) and the exception (one person, in signal red, breaking a perfect system). Structure 2 explains more but has no turn; 3 shows craft but no reason to buy.

## 2. Logline and arc

Every gift idea on the list is cut; what's left is the one person you love, and a whole magazine cover assembles around them.

- Setup (0–6 s): the generic gift list, neat on the grid, cut away.
- Turn (6–10 s): silence; one red square, off grid. "למי שאוהבים, מגיע שער."
- Development (10–24 s): the programme builds the cover around the red square, cover line by cover line, then the same programme makes many issues (a wall), the red one in the middle.
- Ending (24–32 s): push into the red, wipe to the end card: logo, slogan, price, WhatsApp.

## 3. Benchmark

- Swiss identity systems in motion and 1960s Zurich concert posters (grammar only): flush alignment, scale contrast, cuts on the beat, one signal colour.
- Fashion-magazine covers (structure only): masthead, cover lines with page numbers, one huge headline word.
- Learn: precise ease, big white space, giant headline next to tiny notes, sound that is dry and close.
- Don't take: any poster composition, real magazine names, mastheads, typefaces (Helvetica/Akzidenz) or tunes.

## 4. Look

- Page `#F6F1EE` (warm paper), paper `#FFFFFF`, ink `#3A0610` (oxblood, the brand's darkest), greys `#E7DEDB` / `#D5C9C5` / `#9C8C88`, **signal `#D7191F`** (brand cherry red). Only the person (the red square, then the red cover, then the logo) carries the signal.
- One family: **Heebo** (OFL) 300 / 500 / 800 / 900. Hebrew is set **flush right, ragged left** (the Swiss flush-left rule mirrored for RTL).
- Grid: 6 columns, 72 px margins, 24 px gutters, 24 px baseline on 1080×1920.
- The logo (gloss-logo-red.png, and the brand's pink variant on the red cover) is placed as delivered, never recoloured.

## 7. Cue map

120 BPM, 4/4, 16 bars. Synthesised, dry, close.

| Bars | Time | Music state |
|---|---|---|
| 1–3 | 0–6 | tick grid (16ths, filtered), marimba ostinato, soft kick on 1 and 3 |
| 4 | 6–6.5 | total silence (tails cut) |
| 4–5 | 6.5–10 | the exception sings: a sine glide following the square's height, warm pad under |
| 6–9 | 10–18 | motorik kit enters on 10.0; one voice added per cover line; bass from 14.0 (headline) |
| 10–12 | 18–24 | full groove, glockenspiel on the ripple notes |
| 13 | 24–26 | riser into the push, drop on 25.0 |
| 14–15 | 26–30 | thinned: marimba + kick, CTA snap on 27.5 |
| 16 | 30–32 | final chord on 30.0, ring out |

## 9. Type and subtitles

There is no narration, so there are no subtitles; every line is layout type, clip-revealed from below its baseline, held long enough to read (checked by `readcheck`). Lead-ins Heebo 300 in grey, statements Heebo 800 in ink, the cover headline Heebo 900 at ~330 px. Caption rows: Heebo 500 at 26 px with a 1 px rule above.
